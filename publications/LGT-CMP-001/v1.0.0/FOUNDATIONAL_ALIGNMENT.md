# FOUNDATIONAL ALIGNMENT

LGT-CMP-001 v1.0.0 preserves the Living Guide Technology constitutional boundary that a Guide is an accountable companion rather than an authority, oracle, or therapist.

The stable publication closes the publication without expanding CMP beyond its demonstrated responsibility: relational behavior and interaction continuity that remain subordinate to evidence, uncertainty, correction, member agency, permission, and the bounded responsibilities of the wider LGT architecture.

The post-v0.14.0 audit found no evidence-based need for another substantive CMP section.
