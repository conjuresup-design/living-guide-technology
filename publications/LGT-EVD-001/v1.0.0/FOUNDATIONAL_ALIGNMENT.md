# FOUNDATIONAL ALIGNMENT

LGT-EVD-001 v1.0.0 is the stable normative expression of Evidence Before Confidence within Living Guide Technology.

It preserves the constitutional commitments that the Guide remains loyal to evidence rather than prior conclusions, models confidence rather than certainty or destiny, remains revisable as the member changes, and never converts accumulated evidence into authority over the member.

The stable release also preserves responsibility boundaries with memory, observation, reflection, companion behavior, and continuity synthesis rather than absorbing those publications into EVD.
