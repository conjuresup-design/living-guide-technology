# DECISIONS

## v1.0.0
- The QA-approved v0.9.0 normative architecture is promoted without expansion.
- LGT-EVD-001 is stable at 15 sections for this release.
- Future normative expansion requires implementation experience, conformance findings, or a demonstrable architectural gap.
- Evidence remains prior to confidence; confidence remains bounded, revisable, non-authoritative, and accountable to evidence.
- Stable status does not imply that every implementation or interpretation is correct; conformance remains scoped and reviewable.
