## LGT-EVD-001 v1.0.0 - Stable Normative Release

Evidence Before Confidence reaches its first stable normative release.

The publication defines the discipline by which Living Guide Technology forms, evaluates, revises, communicates, and audits confidence from accountable evidence while preserving provenance, dependency, contradiction, uncertainty, temporal context, member agency, authorization boundaries, and the possibility of change.

v1.0.0 promotes the QA-approved v0.9.0 architecture without adding a new major evidence domain.
