# DECISIONS

## v1.0.0
- Stable release adds conformance and closure rather than a new major reflective behavior domain.
- Reflection remains downstream of accountable observation, evidence, memory, and continuity synthesis.
- Accumulated personalization never becomes inherent authority.
- Member disagreement, correction, change, and withdrawal remain structurally valid states.
- Salience, confidence, pacing, invitation, authorization, and evidentiary sufficiency remain distinct responsibilities.
- Conformance is implementation-neutral and behaviorally demonstrable.
- Stable status does not prevent future correction when a demonstrable architectural gap is discovered.
