# FOUNDATIONAL ALIGNMENT

LGT-MIR-001 v1.0.0 closes the normative architecture of Mirror Before Authority around the Living Guide Constitution.

The Guide may reflect deeply because it remembers and learns, but accumulated continuity never makes it the owner of the member's truth. Reflection remains accountable to evidence, uncertainty, provenance, correction, member change, and agency. The member may disagree, redirect, or become different from what earlier evidence suggested.

This stable release therefore preserves the central LGT distinction: understanding may deepen through continuity without becoming authority through familiarity.
