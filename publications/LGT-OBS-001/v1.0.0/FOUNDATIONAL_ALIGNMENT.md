# FOUNDATIONAL ALIGNMENT

LGT-OBS-001 v1.0.0 is the first final release of Observation Before Interpretation.

The publication operationalizes the Living Guide Technology requirement that evidence remain distinguishable from observation and observation remain distinguishable from interpretation. It preserves provenance, temporal and contextual bounds, source identity, uncertainty, revisability, member agency, authorization, auditability, and the person's capacity to change.

Its responsibility boundaries with LGT-MEM-001, LGT-EVD-001, LGT-MIR-001, and LGT-CMP-001 remain explicit.
