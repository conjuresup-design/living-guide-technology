- This is an evolution of the existing Constitution, not a replacement constitution.
- The original twelve-article organization remains intact.
- Later LGT publications may clarify constitutional meaning when the clarification has become foundational across the architecture.
- Subordinate implementation detail remains outside the Constitution.
