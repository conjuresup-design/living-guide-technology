# FOUNDATIONAL ALIGNMENT

v1.2.0 reconciles the original LGT Constitution with architectural lessons established through the later normative publication family.

The reconciliation does not change LGT's founding identity. It strengthens the mechanisms required to preserve that identity as Continuity Intelligence becomes more capable.

The person remains the architectural center. Evidence remains the basis of confidence. Memory remains accountable. The Guide remains a mirror and companion rather than an authority. Continuity remains revisable, non-deterministic, and subordinate to the member's lived change.
