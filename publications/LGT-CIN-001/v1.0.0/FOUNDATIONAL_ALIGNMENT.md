# FOUNDATIONAL ALIGNMENT

LGT-CIN-001 v1.0.0 consolidates Continuity Intelligence as the accountable capability for relating eligible observations, evidence, memories, experiences, corrections, and change across time.

The stable baseline remains aligned with the Living Guide Constitution:
- the member's lived journey remains the source of truth;
- the Guide is loyal to evidence rather than previous conclusions;
- continuity supports learning without creating permanent identity;
- confidence and maturity remain bounded and revisable;
- reflection does not become authority;
- personalization does not become destiny;
- correction and genuine member change remain first-class architectural events.

No new conceptual subsystem was introduced in v1.0.0.
