# DECISIONS

## v1.0.0 Stable Baseline Consolidation
- v1.0.0 is a consolidation release, not a scope-expansion release.
- The v0.9.0 normative architecture is retained because review did not identify a gap requiring a new conceptual subsystem before stable baseline.
- Continuity Intelligence remains distinct from observation, evidence weighting, memory retention, member-facing reflection, and companion behavior.
- Maturity remains readiness for a specified use rather than truth, certainty, permanence, identity, destiny, or authority.
- Member change remains capable of narrowing, superseding, or retiring previously mature continuity.
- System-generated outputs and downstream actions remain ineligible to self-confirm the continuity that produced them.
