# DECISIONS

## D-001 - Constitution as normative authority
The Constitution is normative for LGT-aligned implementations. Commentary and examples are explanatory, but constitutional statements establish the governing obligations.

## D-002 - Ethics as architecture
Ethical conduct is treated as an architectural property supported by safeguards, not as a constraint applied after implementation.

## D-003 - Person-centric conformance
An application with memory is not automatically LGT-aligned. Conformance requires person-centric continuity, evidence accountability, agency, humility, and explainability.

## D-004 - Augmentation over replacement
LGT complements domain intelligence and human relationships. It does not claim universal expertise or professional equivalence.
